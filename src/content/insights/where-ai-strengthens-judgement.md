AI is valuable when it expands the quality or speed of useful work. It becomes risky when fluent output is mistaken for reliable judgement.

## Where AI helps

AI can accelerate early exploration, organise a large body of material, identify patterns for review and help teams test alternative ways of framing a problem.

## Where human judgement remains central

Accountability cannot be delegated. People must decide whether the evidence is sufficient, whether the context has been understood and whether a proposed action is responsible.

## Design the workflow, not just the prompt

A robust AI-enabled workflow defines the input evidence, review checkpoints, prohibited uses and the person accountable for the final decision. The prompt is only one small part of that system.
