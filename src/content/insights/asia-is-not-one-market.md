“Asia” is useful shorthand, but it is a dangerous operating assumption. A regional growth thesis can be sound while the route to growth differs substantially from one country to another.

## Start with the decision

Before gathering more market information, clarify the decision the organisation is trying to make. Is the question whether to enter, where to enter first, which proposition to adapt, or how to build a route to market?

## Separate transferable strengths from local assumptions

Capabilities, brand assets and operating disciplines may travel. Consumer meaning, channel dynamics, price expectations and partnership norms often require local evidence.

## Treat uncertainty as a research agenda

The strongest entry plans make assumptions visible. They identify which beliefs are supported, which are plausible and which could materially change the decision if proven wrong.

The goal is not to eliminate uncertainty. It is to understand which uncertainty deserves attention before the organisation commits.
